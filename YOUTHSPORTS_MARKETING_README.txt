YouthSports.team - MARKETING README
=========================

What Is YouthSports.team?
----------------
YouthSports.team is a calm, parent-first platform for managing youth sports.

It replaces:
- Group chats
- Spreadsheets
- Venmo requests
- Email chains
- Paper forms

All in one simple place.

Who It Is For
-------------
- Youth sports parents
- Schools with multiple sports
- Clubs and AAU programs
- Academies and leagues

What Parents Get
----------------
- One place for schedules, payments, and updates
- Clear next steps for every season
- Easy RSVP and attendance
- No digging through messages
- No missed deadlines

What Coaches Get
----------------
- Clean rosters
- Attendance visibility
- Simple announcements
- Less admin work

What Admins Get
---------------
- Payments tracked properly
- Fewer questions from parents
- Centralized team data
- Multi-sport support

Brand Positioning
-----------------
YouthSports.team is not hype-driven.
It is calm, trustworthy, and practical.

Think:
- Apple Calendar
- Venmo
- Airbnb

Not:
- Social networks
- Loud sports apps
- Complex admin portals

Core Promise
------------
Parents open YouthSports.team for 5 seconds and know exactly what to do.

That is the product.

This file defines the public-facing story of YouthSports.team.
